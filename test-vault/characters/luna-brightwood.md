# Luna Brightwood

**Full Name:** Luna Brightwood
**Race:** Human
**Age:** 22
**Class:** Wizard (School of Divination)
**Background:** Acolyte (Former Apprentice at [[The Argent Tower]])
**Alignment:** Neutral Good
**Party:** [[The Iron Vanguard]]

## Description

Luna is a young woman with bright blonde hair and piercing blue eyes that seem to look through people. She carries herself with the confidence of someone who has seen glimpses of the future and knows that nothing is truly set in stone. Her robes are adorned with silver stars, and she always carries a small crystal ball that she uses for scrying.

> "I've seen a thousand possible futures. In most of them, we win. But winning isn't guaranteed — it's earned."
> — Luna Brightwood

## Background

Luna was born with an innate connection to the Weave of magic, making her a natural spellcaster. She was taken in by the [[The Argent Tower]] at age 10 and quickly rose through the ranks of apprentices. However, she grew frustrated with the tower's academic approach to magic and sought adventure with the [[The Iron Vanguard]].

### Relationships

- **Mentor:** [[Elder Mage Sylas]] — still teaches her advanced spellcraft
- **Rival:** [[Archmage Kaelthorn]] — disapproves of her leaving the tower
- **Ally:** [[The Iron Vanguard]] — her found family
- **Friend:** [[Dr. Aldric Hemlock]] — apothecary who helps with potion ingredients
