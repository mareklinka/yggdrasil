# The Iron Vanguard

**Party Type:** Adventuring Party
**Members:** 5
**Current Level:** 12
**Alignment:** Chaotic Good
**Base of Operations:** [[The Golden Bazaar]], [[Capital City]]

## Overview

The Iron Vanguard is a diverse group of adventurers who have been working together for over two years. Named after their founder's signature weapon, the party has gained a reputation for taking on dangerous missions that others refuse. They are known for their unconventional tactics and unwavering loyalty to each other.

> "We don't always do things the right way. But we always do them together."
> — Captain Elara Thorn

## Party History

### Session 1-5: The Spice Route
The party formed while investigating sabotage on the western trade routes. They discovered a conspiracy involving the [[Royal Merchant Guild]] and the [[Free Cities of the Coast]].

### Session 6-10: The Sunken Depths
The party explored the ruins of [[Aethelgard]] and recovered several artifacts, including fragments of the [[Crown of Aethelgard]]. They encountered the [[Deep Sea Leviathan]].

### Session 11-15: The Iron Corruption
The party has been investigating the [[Iron Corruption]] cult and their creation of undead minotaurs. They have uncovered connections to the ancient [[Iron Heart]] artifact.

## Notable Achievements

- Defeated a [[Greater Dragon]] (Red Dragon variant) in Session 8
- Recovered the [[Crown of Aethelgard]] fragments in Session 12
- Exposed the [[Iron Corruption]] cult's activities in Session 14
- Saved the [[Golden Bazaar]] from a major terrorist attack in Session 15
