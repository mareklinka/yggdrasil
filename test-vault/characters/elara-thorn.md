# Elara Thorn

**Full Name:** Elara Marie Thorn
**Race:** Human
**Age:** 29
**Class:** Ranger (Gloom Stalker)
**Background:** Soldier (Former Captain of the City Guard)
**Alignment:** Chaotic Good
**Party:** [[The Iron Vanguard]]

## Description

Elara is a tall, athletic woman with short brown hair and sharp green eyes that miss nothing. She wears practical leather armor reinforced with steel plates, and her signature longbow is always within reach. A scar runs along her left cheek — a souvenir from her time as a guard captain.

> "I spent ten years following rules. Now I follow my own compass. It points toward justice, not bureaucracy."
> — Elara Thorn

## Background

Elara served as a captain in the [[Capital City]] guard for ten years before resigning in disgust after her superiors covered up corruption involving the [[Royal Merchant Guild]]. She joined the [[Iron Vanguard]] as their tactical leader and has found a new purpose in fighting for what's right rather than what's legal.

### Relationships

- **Leader:** [[The Iron Vanguard]] — she founded and leads the party
- **Mentor:** [[Captain Marcus Reed]] — former commanding officer, now estranged
- **Ally:** [[Guild Master Voss]] — provides information and resources
- **Rival:** [[Lord Percival Blackwood]] — the corrupt noble she tried to expose

## Character Arc

Elara's journey has been one from rigid adherence to law to embracing moral flexibility. She has learned that sometimes the rules prevent justice, and true heroes must be willing to bend or break them when necessary.
