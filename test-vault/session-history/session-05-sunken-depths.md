# Session 5: The Sunken Depths

**Date:** 2026-02-01
**Players:** 4
**DM:** AI
**Duration:** 4.5 hours
**Level:** 6-8

## Summary

The party embarked on their first major dungeon crawl — exploring the ruins of [[Aethelgard]] beneath the [[Shattered Sea]]. This session marked a significant shift in tone and difficulty, introducing underwater combat and environmental hazards.

## Key Events

1. **Preparation:** The party gathered equipment and supplies from the [[Golden Bazaar]]
2. **The Descent:** Used magical breathing spells to reach the ruins at 200 feet
3. **The City Gates:** Found the entrance guarded by ancient [[Stone Golem]] constructs
4. **The Crystal Palace:** Explored the royal residence, fought coral monsters
5. **The Throne Room:** Discovered the last king's journal and a fragment of the [[Crown of Aethelgard]]
6. **The Leviathan Encounter:** Faced a [[Deep Sea Leviathan]] in the deep trenches

## NPCs Introduced

- **King Aldric IV of Aethelgard** — last king, now a ghost
- **High Priestess Lyra** — joined the party temporarily for this session
- **Deep Sea Leviathan** — major boss encounter
- **Aethelgardian Guardian** — ancient construct, boss fight

## Locations Explored

- [[Sunken Depths]] — primary dungeon
- [[Aethelgard]] — ancient city ruins
- The Crystal Palace — royal residence
- The Throne Room — final area
- The Deep Trenches — leviathan encounter
