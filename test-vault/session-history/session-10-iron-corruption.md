# Session 10: The Iron Corruption

**Date:** 2026-02-10
**Players:** 4
**DM:** AI
**Duration:** 5 hours
**Level:** 10-12

## Summary

The party traveled to the [[Ironpeak Mountains]] to investigate the [[Iron Corruption]] cult's activities. This session introduced the cult's terrifying creations — the [[Iron Corrupted Minotaur]] — and revealed the existence of the [[Iron Heart]] artifact.

## Key Events

1. **Arrival in Forgehold:** The party met with [[Thorin Ironforge]] who requested their help
2. **The Corrupted Mines:** Discovered mines that had been taken over by the cult
3. **First Minotaur Encounter:** Faced their first [[Iron Corrupted Minotaur]]
4. **The Cult's Lair:** Infiltrated the cult's underground fortress
5. **The Iron Heart:** Discovered the source of the corruption — a massive iron artifact
6. **Escape:** Fled the collapsing fortress with critical information

## NPCs Introduced

- **Thorin Ironforge** — master blacksmith, ally
- **Brenna Stonehand** — Thorin's apprentice, companion
- **Cult Leader Malachar** — main antagonist of this arc
- **Iron Corrupted Minotaurs (3)** — elite cult warriors
