# Session 15: The Final Stand

**Date:** 2026-03-01
**Players:** 4
**DM:** AI
**Duration:** 6 hours
**Level:** 12

## Summary

The climactic session where the [[The Iron Vanguard]] launched their assault on the [[Iron Corruption]] cult's main fortress to destroy the [[Iron Heart]] artifact. This session featured multiple combat encounters, puzzle-solving, and emotional character moments.

## Key Events

1. **The Plan:** The party devised a multi-pronged assault on the cult's fortress
2. **Infiltration:** [[Mira Cross]] and [[Elara Thorn]] created a distraction at the main gate
3. **The Inner Sanctum:** [[Luna Brightwood]] and [[Brother Marcus]] fought through the cult's elite guards
4. **The Iron Heart Chamber:** The final confrontation with [[Cult Leader Malachar]] and the [[Iron Heart]]
5. **The Sacrifice:** [[Brother Marcus]] used a powerful healing spell to protect the party from the Iron Heart's corruption
6. **The Destruction:** The Iron Heart was destroyed, ending the corruption

## NPCs Introduced

- **Cult Leader Malachar** — final boss, corrupted by the Iron Heart
- **Iron Corrupted Minotaur (Elite)** — Malachar's personal guard
