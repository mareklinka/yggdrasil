# Session 1: The Spice Route

**Date:** 2026-01-05
**Players:** 4
**DM:** AI
**Duration:** 3 hours
**Level:** 1-3

## Summary

The first session introduced the party to the world of the [[Ironhold Kingdom]] and the politics surrounding the [[Royal Merchant Guild]]. The players were introduced to [[The Iron Vanguard]] and their base of operations in the [[Capital City]].

## Key Events

1. **Character Introductions:** Each player introduced their character and backstory
2. **The Spice Contract:** [[Guild Master Voss]] hired the party to investigate sabotage on western trade routes
3. **First Combat:** Ambushed by bandits on the road to [[Golden Bazaar]]
4. **Discovery:** Found evidence pointing to a conspiracy within the guild
5. **Cliffhanger:** A mysterious figure was seen watching the party from the shadows

## NPCs Introduced

- [[Guild Master Voss]] — hired the party
- [[Captain Elara Thorn]] — party leader, former guard captain
- **Bandit Leader "Red" Jake** — first combat encounter
- **Mysterious Stranger** — unknown identity, possibly [[The Whisper Network]]

## Locations Explored

- [[Capital City]] — starting location
- [[Golden Bazaar]] — first major exploration
- Western Trade Route — combat encounter
