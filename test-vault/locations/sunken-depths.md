# The Sunken Depths

**Type:** Underwater Ruins / Dungeon
**Location:** Off the coast of [[Spellhaven]], [[Ironhold Kingdom]]
**Region:** The Shattered Sea
**Depth:** 200-500 feet below sea level

## Overview

The Sunken Depths are the remnants of the ancient civilization of [[Aethelgard]], a once-great kingdom that was swallowed by the sea during the [[Cataclysm of Tears]] over a thousand years ago. The ruins stretch across hundreds of square miles and are filled with magical anomalies, dangerous creatures, and untold treasures.

> "The sea keeps what it takes, and what it keeps, it guards with fury. Enter the Depths at your peril."
> — Ancient Aethelgardian warning, translated

## Geography

### The Surface Approach
The entrance to the Sunken Depths is marked by a ring of ancient stone pillars that rise just above the water's surface. These pillars are inscribed with runes that glow faintly blue when approached by those with pure intentions.

### The First Descent
Upon passing through the pillars, adventurers descend into a massive underwater canyon. The walls are covered in bioluminescent organisms that provide minimal light. Currents are unpredictable and can sweep unwary divers into deeper sections.

### The City of Aethelgard
At the bottom lies the preserved ruins of the ancient capital. Buildings stand in various states of decay, and magical fields preserve certain areas as they were at the moment of destruction.

## Hazards

- **Pressure Zones:** Areas where water pressure increases dramatically
- **Magic Storms:** Chaotic magical fields that can warp reality
- **Aethelgardian Guardians:** Animated constructs that still protect the city
- **Deep Sea Creatures:** Various aquatic predators and aberrations
- **Curse Effects:** Ancient curses that affect those who disturb the ruins

## Notable Artifacts

- **The Crown of Aethelgard:** Royal crown with powerful magical properties
- **The Trident of Poseidon:** Weapon of the ancient sea god
- **The Aethelgardian Codex:** Complete record of the civilization's knowledge

## Adventure Hooks

1. Retrieve the [[Crown of Aethelgard]] before it falls into wrong hands
2. Investigate why the magical anomalies are increasing
3. Rescue a group of adventurers who went in and never returned
4. Seal the ancient curses before they spread to the surface
