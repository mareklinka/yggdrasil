# Forgehold

**Type:** Dwarven City / Underground Metropolis
**Location:** Deep within the [[Ironpeak Mountains]]
**Region:** Northern Continent
**Population:** ~25,000 dwarves

## Overview

Forgehold is the largest and most industrious dwarven city in the [[Ironhold Kingdom]]. Carved into the heart of a mountain, the city is a marvel of dwarven engineering — a labyrinth of forges, tunnels, and chambers that glow with the light of a thousand furnaces. The air is thick with the smell of metal and the sound of hammering.

> "In Forgehold, the mountain doesn't just give us shelter. It gives us purpose. Every strike of the hammer is a prayer to the stone."
> — Thorin Ironforge

## Districts

### The Great Forge
The heart of the city, where the eternal flame burns. This massive chamber houses the largest forge in the world and is where the most important dwarven artifacts are created.

### The Clan Halls
Each dwarven clan has its own hall, decorated with tapestries and monuments to their ancestors. The most prominent include:
- [[Ironforge Clan Hall]] — Thorin's clan
- Stonehand Clan Hall — known for masonry
- Bronzebeard Clan Hall — famous for metalwork

### The Market Depths
The commercial heart of Forgehold, where goods from across the mountains are traded. Known for:
- Rare metals and gemstones
- Dwarven craftsmanship
- Underground taverns and inns
