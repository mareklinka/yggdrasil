# The Golden Bazaar

**Type:** Marketplace / Commercial District
**Location:** [[Capital City]], [[Ironhold Kingdom]]
**Region:** Central Plains
**Population:** ~50,000 (daily visitors)

## Overview

The Golden Bazaar is the largest and most vibrant marketplace in the known world. Spanning over 200 acres in the heart of the [[Capital City]], it is a sprawling labyrinth of stalls, shops, and permanent structures where merchants from every civilization gather to trade goods, information, and secrets.

> "Step into the Bazaar and you'll find everything from the rarest spice to the deadliest poison. Just remember — nothing here is free, and everything has a price."
> — Street vendor's greeting

## Districts

### The Spice Quarter
Home to over 200 spice merchants, the Spice Quarter fills the air with intoxicating aromas. Famous for:
- [[Dragon's Breath Pepper]] — worth its weight in gold
- [[Moonleaf Tea]] — said to enhance magical sensitivity
- [[Sunstone Saffron]] — used in healing potions

### The Armorer's Row
A heavily fortified section where weapons and armor are traded. Known for:
- [[Thorin Ironforge]]'s specialty commissions
- Black market weapons dealers (illegal but tolerated)
- Enchanted weapon appraisers

### The Alchemist's Lane
Home to potion makers and alchemical suppliers. Notable establishments:
- [[The Elixir Emporium]] — high-end potions
- [[Brew & Dash]] — cheap, questionable quality
- [[The Alchemical Exchange]] — rare ingredients

### The Information Exchange
A shadowy corner where information is bought and sold. Famous for:
- [[The Whisper Network]] headquarters
- Bounty hunters and mercenaries
- Smugglers and informants
