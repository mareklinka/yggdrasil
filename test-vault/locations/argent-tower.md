# The Argent Tower

**Type:** Magical Academy / Research Facility
**Location:** [[Spellhaven]], [[Ironhold Kingdom]]
**Region:** Coastal Cliffs
**Height:** 300 feet (above sea level)

## Overview

The Argent Tower is the oldest and most prestigious magical academy in the known world. Rising from the coastal cliffs of [[Spellhaven]], this ancient structure has stood for over a thousand years, surviving wars, magical catastrophes, and the test of time. Its silver-white stone seems to shimmer with contained magical energy.

> "The Argent Tower does not teach magic. It teaches you to listen to what magic wants to teach you."
> — Inscription above the main entrance

## History

Founded in the year 1 by the first Archmage [[Aldric the Wise]], the tower has served as a center of magical learning and research for millennia. It survived the [[Cataclysm of Tears]] when the surrounding city was destroyed, and has since become the primary institution for magical education in the [[Ironhold Kingdom]].

## Layout

### Ground Floor
- **The Grand Atrium:** Central hall with a floating crystal chandelier
- **The Library of Whispers:** Contains over 100,000 magical texts
- **The Examination Halls:** Where mages are tested and ranked

### Upper Floors
- **Classrooms:** Various levels of magical instruction
- **Research Labs:** Advanced magical experimentation
- **The Observatory:** For astronomical and planar studies
- **The Archive:** Restricted section containing forbidden knowledge

### The Summit
- **The Archmage's Sanctum:** Private quarters of the current Archmage
- **The Ritual Chamber:** Where powerful ceremonies are conducted
- **The Crystal Core:** The tower's heart, a massive magical focusing crystal
