# Ironpeak Mountains

**Type:** Mountain Range / Dwarven Territory
**Location:** Eastern [[Ironhold Kingdom]]
**Region:** Northern Continent
**Elevation:** 8,000-12,000 feet

## Overview

The Ironpeak Mountains are a majestic range of iron-rich peaks that have been home to dwarven civilization for millennia. The mountains are rich in precious metals, gemstones, and rare minerals, making them both a blessing and a curse. The dwarven clans have fought countless wars over the mines, and the scars of those conflicts are still visible today.

> "The mountains give, and the mountains take. Respect the peaks, or they will swallow you whole."
> — Dwarven proverb

## Geography

### The Northern Peaks
The highest and most dangerous section, home to ancient dwarven holds and dragon lairs. Known for:
- [[Forgehold]] — Thorin Ironforge's home city
- The Dragon's Maw — an active volcano
- Ancient burial grounds of the [[Ancient Dwarves]]

### The Central Range
The most populated area, containing the majority of dwarven cities and mines. Features:
- Extensive mining operations
- Trade routes connecting to the surface
- The Dwarven Parliament meeting hall

### The Southern Foothills
The gateway to the mountains from the [[Capital City]]. Contains:
- Border forts and checkpoints
- Trading posts and waystations
- The entrance to the [[Undermarket]]

## Resources

- **Iron Ore:** Abundant, high quality
- **Starfall Ore:** Rare meteorite metal, extremely valuable
- **Gemstones:** Emeralds, rubies, and the rare [[Fireheart Gem]]
- **Mithril:** Trace amounts in the deepest mines
