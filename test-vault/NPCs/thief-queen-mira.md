# Thief Queen Mira

**Full Name:** Mirabella "Mira" Cross
**Race:** Human
**Age:** 31
**Affiliation:** [[Shadow Thieves of Oakhaven]]
**Location:** [[The Undercity]], [[Oakhaven]]

## Description

Mira moves like smoke — silent, unseen, and always somewhere you didn't expect. She wears dark leathers that seem to absorb light, and her face is partially concealed by a silver mask shaped like a raven. Only her sharp green eyes are visible, and they miss nothing.

> "In Oakhaven, the streets talk. The question is who's listening, and what they're willing to sell."
> — Thief Queen Mira

## Background

Born in the slums of [[Oakhaven]], Mira survived by her wits from the age of five. She was taken in by the [[Shadow Thieves]] at age 12 and rose rapidly through their ranks thanks to an uncanny ability to infiltrate seemingly impenetrable locations. At 27, she overthrew the previous queen in a bloodless coup by exposing the leadership's corruption to the entire guild.

### Relationships

- **Protégé:** [[Silas "Quick-Fingers" Moore]] — her second-in-command
- **Enemy:** [[Captain Elara Thorn]] — law enforcement's most wanted rogue
- **Informant:** [[The Whisper Network]] — trades secrets with them
- **Love Interest:** [[Dr. Aldric Hemlock]] — apothecary who knows too much

## Abilities

- **Shadow Step:** Can move through shadows with supernatural speed (once per encounter)
- **Lock Mastery:** Can open any lock without tools
- **Street Wisdom:** Knows everyone in the Undercity
- **Deception:** Master of disguise and misdirection

## Current Involvement

Mira has been hired by an unknown patron to steal the [[Crown of Aethelgard]] from the royal vault during the upcoming [[Festival of Lanterns]]. She needs adventurers who can create a diversion while her team infiltrates the castle.

## Quest Hooks

1. [[The Crown Job]] — Mira needs the party to cause a distraction at the royal vault
2. [[The Betrayal]] — Someone in her guild is selling secrets; help her root them out
3. [[Safehouse Raid]] — Her main safehouse was raided; recover stolen goods
