# Guild Master Voss

**Full Name:** Valerius Voss
**Race:** Human (Half-Elf lineage)
**Age:** 54
**Affiliation:** [[Royal Merchant Guild]]
**Location:** [[Golden Bazaar]], [[Capital City]]

## Description

Guild Master Voss is a portly man with silver-streaked hair and a perpetual smile that doesn't always reach his cold, calculating eyes. He wears robes of deep crimson embroidered with golden scales — the symbol of the Merchant Guild.

> "Every coin has a story, and every story has a price. The trick is knowing which is which."
> — Guild Master Voss

## Background

Voss rose through the ranks of the [[Royal Merchant Guild]] over three decades, starting as a humble spice trader in the eastern provinces. His breakthrough came when he brokered the peace trade agreement between the [[Ironhold Kingdom]] and the [[Free Cities of the Coast]] after the Great Blockade of 1142.

### Relationships

- **Ally:** [[Captain Elara Thorn]] — provides security for high-value shipments
- **Rival:** [[Lord Percival Blackwood]] — competes for control of the spice routes
- **Informant:** [[The Whisper Network]] — guild intelligence division
- **Mentor:** [[Old Man Hemlock]] — retired guild master, now lives in retirement

## Current Involvement

Voss has been secretly funding expeditions into the [[Sunken Depths]] and expects the party to recover artifacts from the lost city of [[Aethelgard]]. He offers 500 gold pieces upfront and 10% of recovered value.
