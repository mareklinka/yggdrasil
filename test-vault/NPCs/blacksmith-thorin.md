# Thorin Ironforge

**Full Name:** Thorin Ironforge III
**Race:** Mountain Dwarf
**Age:** 178
**Affiliation:** [[Ironforge Smithy]], [[Dwarven Clan of the Peak]]
**Location:** [[Ironpeak Mountains]], [[Forgehold]]

## Description

Thorin is a broad-shouldered dwarf with a beard so thick and braided it nearly reaches his belt. His arms are corded with muscle and scarred from decades at the forge. He wears a leather apron stained with soot and oil, and his hammer — named "Justice" — is always within reach. His eyes are a warm amber color, and he has a booming laugh that echoes through the smithy.

> "A blade is only as good as the hand that wields it and the heart that guides it. I forge both."
> — Thorin Ironforge

## Background

Thorin is the third of his name in a long line of master smiths. He inherited his father's smithy in [[Forgehold]] and has spent the last century perfecting his craft. His most famous creation is the [[Blade of Dawnbreaker]], a legendary sword said to glow with inner fire against undead creatures.

### Relationships

- **Apprentice:** [[Brenna Stonehand]] — eager young dwarf with natural talent
- **Rival:** [[Silas Blackwater]] — elven smith who uses "unorthodox" methods
- **Ally:** [[Guild Master Voss]] — primary supplier of rare metals
- **Enemy:** [[The Iron Corruption]] — a cult that seeks to corrupt his forges

## Current Involvement

Thorin has been asked to forge a set of enchanted armor for the party's journey into the [[Sunken Depths]]. He requires rare materials: [[Starfall Ore]] from meteorite fragments, [[Deepsea Pearl]] from the [[Sunken Greens]], and the [[Fireheart Gem]] from the volcanic peaks.

## Crafting Specialties

- **Enchanted Weapons:** +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls
- **Dwarven Plate Armor:** AC 18, advantage on saves against poison
- **Potions of Healing:** Brews high-quality potions at half the cost
- **Artisan's Tools:** Master smith's tools grant +4 to crafting checks
