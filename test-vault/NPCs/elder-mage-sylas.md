# Elder Mage Sylas

**Full Name:** Sylas Moonwhisper
**Race:** High Elf
**Age:** 412 (appears ~60)
**Affiliation:** [[The Argent Tower]], [[Circle of Mages]]
**Location:** [[The Argent Tower]], [[Spellhaven]]

## Description

Sylas is an imposing figure draped in robes that seem to shift between shades of blue and silver, like moonlight on water. His long white hair flows past his shoulders, and his eyes glow with a faint luminescence — a side effect of decades spent channeling raw arcane energy. He leans on a staff carved from a single piece of crystal that hums with contained power.

> "Magic is not a tool to be wielded. It is a river to be navigated, and those who try to command it are always the first to drown."
> — Elder Sylas

## Background

Sylas was born during the Age of Ascendancy, when magic flowed freely through the world. He witnessed the [[Cataclysm of Tears]] firsthand — the event that shattered the [[Crystal Spires]] and reshaped the continent. Since then, he has dedicated his existence to understanding the fundamental nature of magic and preventing another catastrophe.

### Relationships

- **Apprentice:** [[Luna Brightwood]] — young human with extraordinary potential
- **Rival:** [[Archmage Kaelthorn]] — believes magic should be weaponized
- **Ally:** [[The Whisper Network]] — shares intelligence on magical disturbances
- **Mentor:** [[Archmage Aldric]] — his teacher, now deceased

## Current Involvement

Sylas has detected anomalous magical readings emanating from the [[Sunken Depths]] and believes the [[Crown of Aethelgard]] is a key to understanding the source. He has commissioned the party to investigate, offering knowledge of advanced spellcraft in exchange.
