# High Priestess Lyra

**Full Name:** Lyra Dawnbringer
**Race:** Human
**Age:** 45
**Affiliation:** [[Temple of the Dawn]], [[Order of the Sacred Light]]
**Location:** [[Temple of the Dawn]], [[Capital City]]

## Description

Lyra carries herself with the grace of someone who has spent decades in prayer and meditation. Her golden hair is always pulled back in a simple braid, and her eyes hold a warmth that puts even the most suspicious people at ease. She wears the simple white robes of the Temple, adorned only with a golden sun symbol at her chest.

> "The light does not ask for your faith. It simply shines, and it is up to you to open your eyes."
> — High Priestess Lyra

## Background

Lyra was born in a small village in the [[Ironhold Kingdom]] and showed signs of divine connection from a young age. She was taken to the [[Temple of the Dawn]] at age 10 and spent the next 15 years training as a cleric. At 30, she was appointed High Priestess after the previous holder passed away.

### Relationships

- **Deacon:** [[Brother Marcus]] — handles the temple's charitable works
- **Rival:** [[Archmage Kaelthorn]] — disagrees with the temple's stance on magical research
- **Ally:** [[Guild Master Voss]] — temple receives significant donations from the guild
- **Mentor:** [[High Priestess Elara]] — her predecessor, now retired

## Current Involvement

Lyra has been troubled by increasing darkness in the [[Sunken Depths]] and believes it is connected to the [[Iron Corruption]] cult's activities. She has asked the party to investigate and, if possible, cleanse the corruption with holy magic.
