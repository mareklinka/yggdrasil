# Greater Dragon

**Type:** Dragon (Aberrant)
**Challenge Rating:** 16
**Size:** Gargantuan
**Alignment:** Any
**CR Range:** 16-23 (depending on age)

## Overview

Greater Dragons are the apex predators of the known world, ancient wyrms of immense power and intelligence. Unlike their lesser cousins, Greater Dragons possess true magical abilities that rival those of the most powerful mages. They are feared and revered across all civilizations.

> "When you hear the shadow pass over the sun, do not look up in wonder. Look up in terror, for the age of mortals is ending."
> — Ancient dragon warning

## Types

### Red Dragon
- **Breath Weapon:** Fire (150ft cone, 20d6 damage)
- **Special Ability:** Fire Resistance, Intimidating Presence
- **Habitat:** Volcanic regions, [[Dragon's Maw]]
- **Temperament:** Aggressive, greedy, destructive

### Blue Dragon
- **Breath Weapon:** Lightning (150ft line, 20d6 damage)
- **Special Ability:** Lightning Resistance, Storm Calling
- **Habitat:** Coastal areas, open plains
- **Temperament:** Calculating, cruel, strategic

### Green Dragon
- **Breath Weapon:** Poison Gas (150ft cone, 20d6 damage)
- **Special Ability:** Poison Resistance, Deception
- **Habitat:** Dense forests, swamps
- **Temperament:** Manipulative, patient, cunning

### White Dragon
- **Breath Weapon:** Cold (150ft cone, 20d6 damage)
- **Special Ability:** Cold Resistance, Ice Walking
- **Habitat:** Frozen peaks, arctic regions
- **Temperament:** Solitary, predatory, instinctual

## Abilities

| Ability | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Legendary Resistance | 3/day, succeed on failed save |
| Magic Resistance | Advantage on saves against spells |
| Lair Actions | Can manipulate surrounding environment |
| Multiattack | Bite + 2 claws + tail slam |
| Flyby | Fly without provoking opportunity attacks |

## Combat Tactics

Greater Dragons are incredibly intelligent and adapt their tactics to each situation:
1. **Opening:** Use breath weapon or magical abilities to weaken opponents
2. **Mid-fight:** Fly and use hit-and-run tactics
3. **Desperation:** Unleash all magical abilities and call for help
4. **Retreat:** If wounded, flee and return with advantage

## Treasure

Greater Dragons accumulate vast hoards over their long lives:
- Gold and precious metals (10,000-100,000 gp value)
- Magical items (5-15 items of varying power)
- Artifacts of immense power (1-3 legendary items)
- Rare materials and components

## Adventure Hooks

1. A red dragon has begun terrorizing villages near [[Forgehold]]
2. A blue dragon has claimed a coastal city as its territory
3. Ancient dragon bones have been found in the [[Ironpeak Mountains]]
4. A dragon egg has been discovered — who laid it and why?
