# Stone Golem

**Type:** Construct
**Challenge Rating:** 10
**Size:** Large
**Alignment:** Unaligned
**CR Range:** 10-15 (depending on enchantment)

## Overview

Stone Golems are massive constructs created by powerful wizards and divine clerics to serve as guardians, warriors, or laborers. Made from living stone infused with magical energy, they are nearly indestructible and follow their commands without question.

> "The stone remembers what the flesh forgets. A golem does not tire, does not falter, does not forget its purpose."
> — Inscription on a golem base

## Creation

Creating a stone golem requires:
- **Materials:** 10,000 gp worth of stone, gemstones, and magical components
- **Time:** 30 days of uninterrupted work
- **Requirements:** Craft Wondrous Item feat, Create Golem feat
- **Spell:** *Greater Create Construct*

## Abilities

| Ability | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Stone Skin | Resistance to non-magical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing |
| Immunity | Immune to poison, disease, and mind-altering effects |
| Magic Resistance | Advantage on saves against spells and magical effects |
| Unyielding | Cannot be frightened or charmed |
| Slam Attack | +8 to hit, 2d10+6 bludgeoning damage per hit |

## Variants

### Marble Golem
- **AC:** 17
- **Special:** Can blend into architectural environments
- **Use:** Indoor guardians, palace guards

### Granite Golem
- **AC:** 19
- **Special:** Higher damage output
- **Use:** Fortress defenders, siege engines

### Obsidian Golem
- **AC:** 18
- **Special:** Can become invisible when not attacking
- **Use:** Stealth operations, assassination

## Weaknesses

- **Shattering Blow:** Critical hits deal double damage
- **Magic Vulnerability:** Vulnerable to disintegrate and similar effects
- **Command Word:** Can be controlled by the creator's command word
- **Destruction:** Requires specific ritual to permanently destroy
