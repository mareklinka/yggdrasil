# Iron Corrupted Minotaur

**Type:** Undead (Minotaur)
**Challenge Rating:** 8
**Size:** Large
**Alignment:** Chaotic Evil
**CR Range:** 8-12 (depending on corruption level)

## Overview

The Iron Corrupted Minotaurs are terrifying undead creatures born from the [[Iron Corruption]] cult's dark rituals. Originally noble dwarven warriors, they have been twisted by an ancient iron-based curse that merges their flesh with living metal. They serve as the cult's elite shock troops in the [[Ironpeak Mountains]].

> "What was once flesh is now iron. What was once life is now curse. What was once noble is now monster."
> — Last words of Clan Leader Thrain, before corruption

## Origin

During the [[Cataclysm of Tears]], an ancient iron artifact known as the [[Iron Heart]] was buried deep within the mountains. The [[Iron Corruption]] cult rediscovered it and began performing rituals to harness its power. The first minotaurs were volunteers who sought to gain the strength to protect their clans — but the Iron Heart consumed their souls and twisted their bodies.

## Corruption Effects

The iron corruption affects creatures in stages:

### Stage 1: Iron Touch
- Skin takes on a metallic sheen
- Immunity to poison and disease
- No combat penalty

### Stage 2: Iron Heart
- Heart replaced by crystallized iron
- Resistance to all physical damage
- Disadvantage on Charisma checks

### Stage 3: Iron Soul
- Soul bound to the [[Iron Heart]]
- Immune to death effects
- Cannot be turned by clerics

### Stage 4: Iron Form
- Complete transformation into undead construct
- Can only be destroyed by destroying the [[Iron Heart]]
- Regenerates 10 HP per round

## Weaknesses

- **Holy Water:** Deals 2d6 radiant damage on contact
- **Magic Weapons:** Must be enchanted to hit
- **Psychic Damage:** Only type of damage that bypasses resistance
- **The Iron Heart:** Destroying it destroys all connected minotaurs
