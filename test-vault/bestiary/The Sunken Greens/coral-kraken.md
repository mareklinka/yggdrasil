# Coral Kraken

**Type:** Aberration (Aquatic)
**Challenge Rating:** 12
**Size:** Large
**Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral
**CR Range:** 10-14 (depending on size)

## Overview

The Coral Kraken is a unique variant of the common kraken that has adapted to life among the coral reefs of the [[Sunken Greens]]. Unlike its deep-sea cousins, the Coral Kraken has developed a symbiotic relationship with the coral ecosystem, using living coral as both camouflage and armor.

> "The reef is alive, and the kraken is its guardian. Disturb the coral, and you disturb the kraken."
> — Merfolk proverb

## Description

Coral Krakens are smaller than their deep-sea relatives, typically reaching 20-30 feet in length. Their tentacles are covered in living coral that grows and regenerates continuously. The coral provides excellent camouflage among the reefs and can deliver toxic spores when threatened.

## Abilities

| Ability | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Coral Armor | Resistance to piercing and slashing damage |
| Tentacle Slam | +7 to hit, 2d8+4 bludgeoning damage |
| Coral Spores | Cloud of toxic spores (15ft radius, DC 14 Con save) |
| Entangle | Tentacles can grapple multiple targets |
| Camouflage | Advantage on Stealth checks among coral reefs |

## Habitat

Coral Krakens are found exclusively in:
- The [[Sunken Greens]] — their primary habitat
- Coral reef systems throughout the [[Shattered Sea]]
- Shallow waters near [[Spellhaven]] coast

## Behavior

Coral Krakens are territorial but not inherently aggressive. They will defend their reef territory fiercely but will generally avoid confrontation with larger creatures. They have been known to form symbiotic relationships with merfolk and other aquatic races.

### Diet
- Small fish and crustaceans
- Plankton and algae
- Occasionally, lost ships and divers

### Intelligence
Coral Krakens have an intelligence of 10 and can understand basic commands. They are known to communicate with merfolk through complex tentacle movements.

## Weaknesses

- **Fire Damage:** Coral is highly flammable
- **Acid Damage:** Dissolves the coral armor
- **Uprooting:** Removing coral from the reef weakens it
- **Sonar:** Disorients their navigation

## Adventure Hooks

1. A Coral Kraken has been attacking divers near [[Spellhaven]]
2. The merfolk need help negotiating with a territorial kraken
3. An invasive species is destroying the coral reefs — the kraken is reacting
4. A rare pearl has been found inside an ancient kraken
