# Deep Sea Leviathan

**Type:** Aberration (Aquatic)
**Challenge Rating:** 14
**Size:** Huge
**Alignment:** Neutral
**CR Range:** 14-18 (depending on size)

## Overview

The Deep Sea Leviathan is a massive aquatic predator that lurks in the darkest depths of the [[Shattered Sea]]. These ancient creatures are among the oldest living beings on the continent, having survived since before the [[Cataclysm of Tears]]. They are both revered and feared by those who navigate the waters near the [[Sunken Depths]].

> "The sea has its kings, and the leviathan is their sovereign. To encounter one is to stand before the ocean's wrath made flesh."
> — Captain's log, unknown author

## Description

Leviathans can reach lengths of up to 80 feet, with bodies covered in thick, barnacle-encrusted scales that range from dark blue to black. Their eyes glow with an eerie bioluminescence, and their mouths are filled with rows of razor-sharp teeth. Tentacles line their jaws, each tipped with sensory organs that can detect the slightest vibration in the water.

## Habitat

Leviathans are found primarily in:
- The [[Sunken Depths]] — their primary hunting ground
- The [[Shattered Sea]] — migratory patterns
- Deep ocean trenches — breeding grounds
- Near [[Spellhaven]] coast — occasionally spotted

## Behavior

Despite their terrifying appearance, leviathans are not inherently aggressive. They are curious creatures that will investigate unusual vibrations in the water. However, they will defend their territory fiercely and view submarines or diving operations as threats.

### Diet
- Large fish and squid
- Sharks and other apex predators
- Occasionally, ships that venture too close

### Intelligence
Leviathans have an intelligence of 12 and can understand basic commands. Ancient legends speak of leviathans that can communicate with merfolk and other aquatic races.

## Weaknesses

- **Sonar Disruption:** Loud noises disorient them
- **Net Mastery:** Special nets can restrain them
- **Harpoon Guns:** Enchanted harpoons can pierce their scales
- **Ancient Songs:** Certain melodies can calm or agitate them

## Adventure Hooks

1. A leviathan has been attacking ships near [[Spellhaven]] — investigate
2. The leviathan is protecting something in the [[Sunken Depths]]
3. An ancient merfolk tribe needs help communicating with a leviathan
4. A leviathan egg has been discovered — whose responsibility is it?
