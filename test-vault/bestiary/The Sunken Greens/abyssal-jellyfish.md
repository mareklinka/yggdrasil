# Abyssal Jellyfish

**Type:** Aberration (Aquatic)
**Challenge Rating:** 6
**Size:** Medium
**Alignment:** Unaligned
**CR Range:** 4-8 (depending on size)

## Overview

The Abyssal Jellyfish is a bioluminescent predator found in the deepest parts of the [[Shattered Sea]]. Despite their gelatinous appearance, they are highly dangerous creatures whose tentacles carry a neurotoxin powerful enough to paralyze a full-grown dwarf.

> "Beautiful, luminous, and utterly deadly. The abyssal jelly is the sea's most elegant assassin."
> — Marine biologist's journal

## Description

Abyssal Jellyfish are translucent creatures that glow with an eerie blue light. Their bells can reach up to 4 feet in diameter, and their tentacles can extend up to 20 feet. The tentacles are lined with stinging cells that release a potent neurotoxin on contact.

## Abilities

| Ability | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Toxic Touch | +5 to hit, 1d8+3 poison damage + paralyzed for 1 round |
| Bioluminescence | Can glow to attract prey or communicate |
| Jelly Body | Resistance to all damage except force |
| Tentacle Whip | Attack up to 10ft away |
| Regeneration | Regenerates 5 HP per round in water |

## Habitat

- Deep ocean trenches
- The [[Sunken Depths]] — particularly near the ruins
- Underwater caves and crevices
- Around the [[Sunken Greens]] coral reefs

## Behavior

Abyssal Jellyfish are solitary creatures that drift through the water column, using their bioluminescence to attract prey. They are not aggressive but will defend themselves if threatened. Their neurotoxin is highly valued by alchemists and poison makers.

### Diet
- Small fish and plankton
- Other jellyfish and aquatic creatures
- Organic debris

### Reproduction
They reproduce asexually through budding, creating clones that drift away to establish new territories.

## Weaknesses

- **Fresh Water:** Causes rapid deterioration
- **Fire Damage:** Burns through tentacles quickly
- **Salt Concentration:** Changes in salinity disorient them
- **Net Capture:** Can be captured with special nets

## Adventure Hooks

1. A colony of abyssal jellyfish has appeared near a diving operation
2. The neurotoxin is being used in assassinations — track the source
3. A rare variant with golden bioluminescence has been spotted
4. The jellyfish are migrating — something is driving them from their habitat
